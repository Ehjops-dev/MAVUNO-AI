## Brand & Style

This design system is rooted in a **Modern Minimalist** aesthetic with a **Tactile** edge. It is designed to evoke high trust, environmental consciousness, and technological sophistication. The interface prioritizes clarity and breathability, moving away from cluttered "tech" layouts toward an editorial, premium experience. 

The target audience consists of institutional investors, sustainability leads, and innovative enterprise partners. The emotional response should be one of "composed confidence"—the feeling of a high-end architectural space where every detail is intentional. 

Key visual principles:
- **Spaciousness:** Generous negative space to reduce cognitive load.
- **Organic Precision:** A blend of sharp grid alignment with soft, 20px radius corners.
- **Luminosity:** High-key light theme that feels fresh and transparent.

## Colors

The palette follows a strict **80/15/5 ratio** to maintain a clean, high-trust professional atmosphere.

- **Primary (#065F46):** A deep Emerald Green used for primary actions, navigation anchors, and authoritative type. It represents stability and growth.
- **Secondary (#F0FDF4):** A soft Mint/Off-white used for large sectional backgrounds and surface containers to provide subtle contrast against pure white (#FFFFFF).
- **Tertiary (#10B981):** A vibrant Emerald used sparingly for accents, data visualization peaks, and success states.
- **Neutral (#6B7280 / #111827):** Slate grays for body text and functional UI elements, ensuring maximum legibility without the harshness of pure black.

Backgrounds should default to pure white (#FFFFFF), using the secondary mint tint only to distinguish between different content modules.

## Typography

The design system utilizes **Inter** exclusively to lean into a systematic, utilitarian, and highly legible aesthetic. 

- **Hierarchy:** Dramatic scale differences between display headings and body text create an editorial feel. 
- **Tight Tracking:** Headlines should use slight negative letter-spacing (-0.01em to -0.02em) to appear more cohesive and "premium."
- **Line Height:** Body text uses a generous 1.6x line height to reinforce the "Calm" brand narrative and ensure effortless reading of technical content.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Use the `label-caps` style for small descriptors, eyebrow headlines, or category tags to create a structured, institutional look.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy is built on a **Fixed Grid** with hyper-extended vertical spacing to emphasize "premiumness."

- **Grid:** A 12-column grid for desktop (1280px max-width) with 32px gutters. On mobile, transition to a single-column layout with 24px side margins.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections are separated by large 160px gaps. This "excessive" white space communicates that the brand is not rushed and values the user's focus.
- **Alignment:** Use asymmetrical layouts (e.g., content spanning columns 2-7) to create visual interest while maintaining the underlying grid integrity.
- **In-Component Spacing:** Use a strict 8px (base) scale for internal padding.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a "Calm" and "Sustainable" feel, elevation is used sparingly to suggest physical presence without looking "heavy."

- **Ambient Shadows:** Surfaces use extra-diffused shadows. For example: `0 10px 40px rgba(6, 95, 70, 0.04)`. Note the slight green tint in the shadow to harmonize with the primary color.
- **Tonal Layering:** Depth is primarily created through subtle background shifts (White to Secondary Mint) rather than heavy shadows.
- **Interactive Depth:** On hover, cards should subtly lift using a slightly more pronounced shadow and a 2px upward translation, suggesting responsiveness and life.

## Shapes

The shape language is the core differentiator of this design system. 

- **Primary Radius:** All main containers, cards, and buttons utilize a consistent **20px corner radius**. This large radius softens the professional Inter typography and deep green palette, making the high-tech subject matter feel approachable and organic.
- **Input Fields:** Search bars and text inputs should follow the 20px radius to match the card language.
- **Buttons:** Buttons are large with 20px corners, creating a distinct "soft rectangle" look rather than a sharp edge or a full pill shape.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald (#065F46) background, White text. 20px radius. No border.
- **Ghost:** Transparent background, Deep Emerald text, and a subtle 1px border at 20% opacity.
- **Sizing:** Large padding (16px 32px) to ensure a high-end tactile feel.

### Cards
- **Feature Cards:** White background, 20px radius, 1px border (#E5E7EB), and a subtle green-tinted ambient shadow. 
- **Padding:** 40px internal padding to maintain the "spacious" brand promise.

### Input Fields
- **Design:** 20px radius, soft mint background (#F0FDF4), and no border in the default state. Upon focus, add a 1px solid Primary Emerald border.

### Chips & Tags
- **Style:** Use the `label-caps` typography. Background should be 10% opacity of the Primary color, with 100% opacity text. Fully rounded (pill) to distinguish them from actionable buttons.

### Navigation
- **Header:** Sticky glassmorphism effect (Backdrop blur: 12px) with a subtle bottom border. High-transparency white background to allow content to scroll underneath beautifully.

### Sustainability Indicators
- A custom component for this system: small, circular progress rings or data micro-visuals using the Tertiary Emerald (#10B981) to represent ESG metrics or growth data.
